# Omi Mail: source and authorship

Omi Mail is an independently branded and maintained domain-mail application.
Its visual identity, mailbox workspace, login experience and additional changes
are developed for this project, maintained at
https://github.com/masoneai/cloud-mail-optimized.

The repository began with [maillab/cloud-mail](https://github.com/maillab/cloud-mail)
at commit `ec7a2bb17c950576c38d7308128cac7696fea169`. Portions of its mail service,
administration features and supporting code remain in use. The repository is
therefore a derivative project with its own design and additional implementation.
Upstream's MIT copyright and permission notice are kept
in [LICENSE](LICENSE), alongside attribution for Omi Mail's additions.

The Omi Mail mark and product copy were created for this project. The decorative
login illustration was generated for this project; it is stored at
`mail-vue/public/image/login-mail-garden.webp`. These assets are distributed
under the repository's MIT license.

Bundled third-party software retains its own notices and licenses. In particular,
TinyMCE's license and notices remain in `mail-vue/public/tinymce/`; its terms
apply independently of this repository's license. Icon libraries and other
dependencies retain their respective licensing terms.
